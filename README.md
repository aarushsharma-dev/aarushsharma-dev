## 🚧 Projects & experiments

These are areas I've been exploring. I'll keep improving the documentation as the implementations become more complete and easier to demonstrate.

### 🎬 ClipStudio — Natural Hinglish Caption Engine
An experiment focused on generating more natural **Hinglish captions** from speech. The goal is to reduce awkward, overly literal transliteration and make captions read more like the way people actually speak.

**Currently exploring:** transcription quality, Hindi–English code-switching, transliteration cleanup, and caption readability.

### 🚗 OnRoad Legal — Highway Legal Assistance Concept
A concept for connecting drivers who need roadside legal assistance with nearby verified advocates. The product idea explores location-aware discovery, real-time communication, and a clear request-and-response flow.

**Currently exploring:** product workflows, secure data handling, realtime updates, and how to make the experience practical for users.
